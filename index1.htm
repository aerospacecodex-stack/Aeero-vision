05 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for a Safer Planet

AeroVision is a space and Earth observation dashboard concept created to make satellite science easier to explore. It brings disaster-awareness workflows, mission information, and educational resources together in a single interface.

  • Earth ObservationLearn how optical, thermal, and radar imagery can help researchers study changes across our planet.
  • Disaster AwarenessExplore example workflows for fire, flood, storm, and post-event impact assessment.
  • Space EducationDiscover selected missions and the science behind satellites, lunar exploration, and solar observation.
  • Future Data IntegrationDesigned for future connection to verified public satellite products, APIs, and geospatial mapping services.
